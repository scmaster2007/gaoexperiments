How these numbers were measured
  • Setup: CIFAR-10 (50,000 train, 10,000 test), flattened standardized pixels, ReLU MLP with PyTorch default init and biases, width 1024 unless noted, squared loss on one-hot labels, 500 epochs. Adam defaults: lr 1e-3, β = (0.9, 0.999), ε = 1e-8, batch 128, constant lr. AdamW weight decay 0.01. SGD momentum 0.9.
  • Collapse epoch: after training accuracy first reaches 0.8, the first checkpoint where it is below 0.2. "Never 0.8" runs are shown as hollow rings.
  • Seed-average test accuracy: accuracy of the outputs averaged over seeds. Agreement: fraction of test images on which two seeds predict the same class, averaged over pairs.
  • Collapsed runs predict one class for every test image, so agreement between two collapsed runs is 1 or 0.
  • Data-order groups (init seed ≠ data-order seed) are kept separate from seed statistics.
  • Curves are downsampled to about 34 checkpoints per run: log-spaced to epoch 10, then every 20 epochs.
  • Inside training quantities come from 13 checkpoints: epochs 0, 1, 3, 10, 25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400, 500. Hessian quantities use 2,048 fixed training images and 30 power iterations; the NTK uses 250 training + 250 test probe images. Preconditioned sharpness is the top eigenvalue of P⁻¹H with P = (1 − β₁ᵗ)(√v̂ + ε).
  • Inactive fraction: share of ReLU units that output 0 on all 1,000 probe test images. Effective rank: exp(entropy) of the normalised squared singular values of the layer's outputs on those images.
  • Training ran in fp32 with TF32 off; saved outputs and weights are fp16.
